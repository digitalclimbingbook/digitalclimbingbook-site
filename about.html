About

--

This was made just for fun, because the developer wanted to track their climbs in an easy to update and visualize database without paying a premium or subscription fee to any other platforms. The goal is to make this accessible to others at scale and provide more enhancements overtime that make having a digital climbing archive fun and valuable for climbers worldwide. The quest generation is intended to be fun and leverage technology for a useful purpose that inspires new routes to be climbed and found in the world. Hope you'll enjoy this application too!

Digital Climbing Book is an independent project and currently in Alpha testing.

If you would like to be included in testing, please reach out to the email address with your information to participate.

- Name
- Social Media Handle (1-2)
- Max Grade Level
- Location

Users will be selected based on the needs for testing.
